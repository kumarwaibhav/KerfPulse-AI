# Engineer and investor demonstration

1. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 and choose Score a New Cut.
2. Load measured example. Inspect new event/tool identity, current features, cut cutoff and planned future break. Source metadata discloses publisher tool 2 and demonstration clock; no future outcome label appears in the request.
3. Validate and score. Show released model version, applicability, five-cut benchmark score and slot-relative actionable score. These scores concern different windows.
4. Approve a recommended review. Enter an inspection finding with source and complete it. Export evidence and immutable model/policy identifiers.
5. Load unsupported example and score. The result has unavailable risk and a qualification reason. Show physical source disconnected state.
6. Open historical Performance and show complete negative benchmark alongside any successful example. Current-only/periodic produced 2/14 timely; selective policy produced1/14 under the nested-workpiece protocol.
7. For audience files, download CSV/JSON templates. Fresh user measurements remain unsupported until raw-to-feature equivalence and commissioning are proven. This boundary is a product capability rather than a fabricated live machine.

Demo goals are inference without a known-cutter runtime lookup, durable evidence and human decision workflow. Physical connection and predictive generalization are separately qualified.

## Suggested five-minute narrative

- **Problem, 30 seconds:** maintenance teams need to turn cutter measurements into a reviewable inspection decision, with enough context to trust or decline it.
- **Fresh evidence, 90 seconds:** submit the measured reference example under a new tool-life identity. Show that the request contains current/context values and a future break, but no damage label. Explain engineering profile and demonstration clock.
- **Decision and traceability, 90 seconds:** compare next-five and slot-window scores, approve review, record an explicitly sourced finding, reload and export original input plus versioned result/history.
- **Failure handling, 45 seconds:** submit unsupported evidence and show unavailable scores. Reuse an event ID with changed values and show quarantine. An abstention is visible, not a green machine-health claim.
- **Research and scale, 45 seconds:** show complete benchmark, negative policy result and measured service test. Separate software delivery metrics from predictive utility.

Do not claim the illustrative high-score example avoided damage. It was selected for workflow demonstration, and its recommendation is not evidence of timely intervention. A five-cut risk output is not continuous RUL, and no during-cut anomaly model is implemented.

## Evidence bundle and judge questions

Open the current checkpoint, claims register, research plan and raw load results when asked about accuracy or readiness. Explain that the model is a transparent research baseline; the proposed scientific contribution is decision-aware evidence/usefulness under domain and action constraints, still requiring comparative validation. Software contribution now demonstrated is new-identity inference with provenance, abstention and durable human workflow. Neither alone establishes journal novelty or plant readiness.

If asked to connect a machine, show unconfigured state and commissioning checklist. If asked to upload another machine's data, demonstrate schema validation and the unsupported-domain result. Never rename a protocol fixture, benchmark replay or repeated load-test vector as a live factory feed.
