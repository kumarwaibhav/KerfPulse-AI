# Claims and limitations

Defensible pitch: KerfPulse turns compatible machining measurements into traceable inspection decision support, with explicit data-quality and model-applicability checks.

The fresh screen accepts new event and tool-life IDs, validates completed-cut summaries, runs one released current model, stores assessments, and records human review. Reference measured examples come from an excluded source group; example clocks are explicitly demonstration timestamps. Arbitrary controller load, amps or other machines are unqualified and receive unavailable scores.

Legacy AP .355 and 2/14 timely are retained nested research results. They do not describe a validated plant probability or exact RUL. The original negative selective-release finding remains visible. Fresh model training is a development release and does not create a new untouched confirmation set.

Physical connection, independent-machine validity, controlled customer access and concurrent production capacity remain qualification milestones. The historical 12-run stress target failed. User evidence and source mapping cannot be declared safe by a graph or by passing unit tests. No autonomous machine-control action exists.

Later local replay regression, after bounded pooling, passed its 600-second gate with 12 concurrent replays: 46,439 events at 77.398/s, p95 SSE 386.826 ms and no harness-reported failures. The earlier failed test is preserved as historical evidence. This improvement closes that measured local replay gate only, not broad concurrent production readiness or physical-device reliability.
