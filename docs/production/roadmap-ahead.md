## Research direction and claim boundary

Candidate mechanism: estimate the expected reduction in inspection-decision loss from optional vibration, conditioned on cheap current evidence and next feasible break. Labels for that estimator come only from cross-fitted development predictions. At inference, the gate sees no unreleased vibration and no future outcome. A hard prefix quota bounds requested evidence; real acquisition costs and transmission costs must remain separate.

Active feature acquisition already has extensive prior work; fusion and a learned gate alone do not establish novelty. The proposed contribution is the explicit combination of causal acquisition, actionable calendar horizon, domain abstention and audited maintenance utility, subject to matched ablations and independent confirmation. [Active feature acquisition survey, revised 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11067).

Fixed illustrative cost ratios in research code are not factory financial estimates. Shared maximum budgets do not imply equal realized sensor acquisitions. Completed-cut forecasts do not establish during-cut anomaly detection or continuous RUL.
