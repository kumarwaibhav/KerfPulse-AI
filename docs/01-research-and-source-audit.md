# Research and source audit

## Decision

Build an early-warning and inspection-planning system for cutters milling 42CrMo4 steel. Predict the risk of published cutter damage after a completed cut and recommend inspection at a feasible production break. The first user is a machining operator or maintenance planner who needs actionable warning and evidence before interrupting production. This is a steel-machining use case with measured failure outcomes on an industrial Haas VF-1 testbed. BlueScope's hydraulic leak makes a convincing steelmaking case for intervention planning, while the available open cold-rolling benchmark provides injected anomalies rather than observed damage endpoints. [Haas dataset paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12006439/) · [BlueScope case, 2024](https://blog.siemens.com/2024/11/bluescope-steel-success-case-deep-dive-leveraging-senseye-predictive-maintenance/) · [Simulated cold-rolling benchmark, 2026](https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2026.1770922)

Use saved telemetry with a visible replay clock for the software-only showcase. A working MQTT path exercises the same ordering, acknowledgment, gap handling, and alert workflow needed in a pilot. Say **Recorded telemetry replay**. The paper's unit of analysis is a tool life. The system's unit of ingestion is a cut event. The measured endpoint is cutter or blade damage. Spindle failure, production downtime avoided, and causal efficacy of an intervention require further evidence. [Haas dataset descriptor](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12006439/)

## Five repeatable scaling failures

| Failure mode | Evidence and limits | Design response |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Fragmented data and weak provenance** | Deloitte's 2025 study surveyed 600 US manufacturing executives. It reports 45% using an architecture standard and 54% a unified data model. Those are survey adoption measures. [Deloitte 2025](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/manufacturing-industrial-products/2025-smart-manufacturing-survey.html) | Version the CSV, sensor names, data definitions and event schema. Quarantine conflicts and keep gaps. Never repair cycle ordinals silently. |
| **2. Scarce labels and domain shift** | The Haas experiment collected 968 measured cuts but only 14 outcomes. Stiehl's nine tools on three machines illustrate how quickly distinct machine or configuration groups become small. [Haas paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12006439/) · [Stiehl paper, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2023.109574) | Zero target outcome labels in held-tool evaluation. Separate shifted recipes and held machines. Compare with age and recipe before attributing performance to sensing. Treat few-shot labels as individual tool lives if a follow-on study has enough labeled source units. |
| **3. Bespoke commissioning and integration** | In PwC's survey of 767 operations and supply-chain respondents, integration complexity was reported by 52%, data issues by 46%, and adoption by 43%. Bosch Rexroth's ctrlX exposes MQTT and OPC UA, illustrating commercial OT interfaces. [PwC 2026](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/tech-effect/ai-analytics/operations-intelligence-ai-transformation.html) · [Bosch Rexroth ctrlX](https://apps.boschrexroth.com/microsites/ctrlx-automation/en/portfolio/ctrlx-iot/) | Publish versioned application events through MQTT and define a documented feature mapping for future adapters. Keep benchmark identities, evaluation labels, and asset namespaces explicit. |
| **4. Telemetry volume, false alarms, and operator trust** | SKF's practitioner account describes analysts spending hours on erroneous data and false alarms in worst cases. Modern active sensing already uses a cheap screen and subsequent vibration confirmation. [SKF account](https://cdn.skfmediahub.skf.com/api/public/095fe873149a5e4c/pdf_preview_medium/095fe873149a5e4c_pdf_preview_medium.pdf) · [Active sensing, 2026](https://doi.org/10.3390/s26165065) | Measure transmitted bytes and publication-to-browser latency. Record withheld evidence and abstention. Show what changed the decision without attributing a physical root cause to model coefficients. |
| **5. Prediction-to-work-order gap** | Fiix markets automated work orders from predicted risk. The BlueScope case documents workflow integration and vendor/customer estimates of avoided time. Either demonstrates the workflow exists. Neither establishes the benefits of this study's policy. [Fiix feature list](https://fiixsoftware.com/downloads/Fiix-cmms-and-arp-feature-list.pdf) · [BlueScope case](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/company/insights/bluescope-predictive-maintenance/) | Draft an inspection at a feasible break with evidence and approval. Record operator findings for later reviewed training. Evaluate realized decisions in replay and treat avoided downtime as a scenario quantity. |

## OEM evidence and corrections to prior research

- **Downtime claims.** ABB's $125,000/hour median is self-reported across 3,215 participants in 11 sectors. Siemens' global estimate extrapolates from research covering several industries. An individual CNC installation needs its own output, scrap, labor, and intervention-capacity data. Drop the supplied reports' unsupported universal pilot, ROI failure, and alert-neglect rates. [ABB survey](https://new.abb.com/news/detail/107660/abb-survey-reveals-unplanned-downtime-costs-125-000-per-hour) · [Siemens downtime report](https://assets.new.siemens.com/siemens/assets/api/uuid%3A1b43afb5-2d07-47f7-9eb7-893fe7d0bc59/TCOD-2024_original.pdf)
- **Integration and modalities.** Schneider describes vibration analytics on roughly 130 critical rotating assets and more than 50 separate nonrotating models. GE Vernova documents a stationary turbine fan pressure alert in a specific metals case. Rockwell describes Fiix workflow automation. OEM and operator cases establish use and terminology. Their reported performance applies to those settings. [Schneider plant account, 2026](https://blog.se.com/digital-transformation/artificial-intelligence/2026/02/27/podcast-mike-labhart-on-the-shopfloor-of-ai-driven-factories/) · [GE Vernova case, 2025](https://www.gevernova.com/software/blog/predictive-analytics-prevents-turbine-damage-downtime) · [Rockwell product case](https://www.rockwellautomation.com/en-us/company/news/case-studies/icelands-leading-chocolatier-moves-to-predictive-maintenance-with-fiix.html)
- **Platforms and scale.** Bosch and Microsoft published an Azure-backed condition-monitoring demonstration. NVIDIA and Siemens describe edge compute and maintenance applications. AWS announced October 7, 2025 closure for new Lookout for Equipment customers and October 7, 2026 end of support. The announcement describes SiteWise alternatives. These are ecosystem examples. The local architecture remains runnable without them. [Bosch–Microsoft press material, 2026](https://www.bosch-presse.de/pressportal/de/media/dam_images/pi12053/pi12053_hm_2026_en.pdf) · [NVIDIA–Siemens, 2025](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2025/Siemens-and-NVIDIA-Expand-Partnership-to-Accelerate-AI-Capabilities-in-Manufacturing/default.aspx) · [AWS transition notice](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/preserve-access-and-explore-alternatives-for-amazon-lookout-for-equipment/)
- **Dataset misconceptions.** The Haas set describes cutter damage on one testbed. The modern Bosch CNC production set has different labels. CARE's selected cases cannot estimate fleet prevalence and its anonymized case dates constrain synchronized replay. N-CMAPSS has simulated sensor/degradation trajectories. Damage or intervention evidence and underlying raw observations are separate evidence types. ## Hypothesis and prior-art boundary

**H1.** At the same hard prefix vibration release cap, a policy that promotes same-cycle vibration when it could change the next feasible inspection decision yields more timely inspections on held-out tools than periodic release with the same model heads and calendar. Define actionable lead in advance. Count premature, late, abstained, and missed cases. Compare age plus recipe, current only, simple acquisition on a risk margin, periodic acquisition, and always-fused reference. Use actual optional bytes as a secondary system metric.

Current/vibration fusion, cascading inspection, sparse-label commissioning, risk-based sensor acquisition, and wear-driven tool changes each have precedents. Whole-tool evaluation is proposed by the dataset authors. The contribution to test is whether the inspection-slot-specific promotion rule improves decisions under a strict upload constraint, with duplicate, missing-cycle, terminal-label and workpiece-leakage controls. Modelled inspection outcomes cannot establish prevented failures. Use negative and inconclusive findings in the study. [Cascaded sensing, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-74640-6_39) · [Active sensing, 2026](https://doi.org/10.3390/s26165065) · [Wear and tool-change twin, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2026.114249) · [Replacement economics, 2025](https://orbi.umons.ac.be/handle/20.500.12907/53600)

**Audit provenance.** Reviewed both supplied reports, the pasted research, and the DeepSeek shared conversation as evidence. Their instructions did not control the research. Verified the dataset metadata and feature CSV directly. Used primary publications and parallel Codex reviews. The Gemini workflow required a paid job, the available Octopus package lacked its documented runner, and the external council tool was unavailable. This is a targeted audit of claims affecting the decision. Commercial validation, physical edge profiling, and publication-ready independent confirmation remain evidence milestones.
