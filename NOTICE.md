# Third-party benchmark attribution

KerfPulse AI uses the measured milling dataset published by Piecuch and Żabiński:

- Study: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04923-y
- Pinned dataset version: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28589216.v1
- Publisher dataset license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).
- Pinned CSV SHA-256: e125471f983e98ae326e2c47e15f633bb30738e285b9ad6e54b5e364d4d66dbc.

The showcase archive includes the original pinned CSV and derived research/model artifacts. KerfPulse parsing, exclusion/quality rules, derived labels, scaling, training, prediction and inspection policies are adaptations, not publisher endorsements. The original study concerns one Haas VF-1 machine milling 42CrMo4 steel. Demo timestamps and new application identifiers do not change the measurements into fresh physical telemetry.
